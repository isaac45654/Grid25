Found Words

How To Play

Tap letters to spell words, and press Submit.

Enter words of 4 or more letters to make the letters disappear.

Remove every letter to complete the puzzle.

If you need to use letters from words you already found, double tap a found word to return it to the board.

If you make a mistake while entering a word, tap a letter to remove it.

There is a new puzzle every day.

The less words you enter the better. Share your score with friends.

Check your streak and data by clicking the statistics button.
